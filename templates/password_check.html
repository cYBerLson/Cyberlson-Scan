Educational Insight: What is Password Entropy?

Entropy is a measure of how unpredictable a password is. It's calculated in bits based on the length of the password and the size of the character pool (lowercase, uppercase, numbers, symbols) it uses. A password with 80+ bits of entropy is generally considered very strong and resistant to brute-force attacks.
